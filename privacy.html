Kotopi privacy policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

Kotopi is a voice diary for children aged 6 to 10, published by Kurage Studios and set up by a parent.

Your data stays on your device

Recordings, children's names, avatars and settings are never sent to us. We have no server and no accounts. In the Android app they are stored on the device (recordings in Documents/Kotopi); on the website, in the browser. Delete recordings in the app at any time; uninstalling the app or clearing the site's data removes everything else.

The microphone is used only while recording. Questions are read aloud by the device's text-to-speech.

No tracking

The Android app has no analytics, ads or crash reporting. The website uses cookie-free Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits anonymously, and is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses for security.

Children

No ads, no purchases, no contact with other people. External links are only in the parent area. We collect no personal information from children.

Changes to this policy will be posted here with a new date.

Terms of use

  1. Kotopi is free for personal and family use, under a parent's supervision.
  2. Recordings are yours and stored only on your device; back them up, as we cannot recover them.
  3. Kotopi is provided "as is", without warranty. To the extent permitted by law, we are not liable for data loss or other damage.
